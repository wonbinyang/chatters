# Chatters

**Chatters** is an ongoing series of small sound-and-light forms by Wonbin Yang.  
These forms inhabit paintings, installations, sculptural scenes, and other environments,  
using simple electronic behaviors to create presence, rhythm, and quiet communication.

This repository serves as an **archive** of the code, structures, and digital materials  
related to the development of the series. Each folder documents a different stage,  
form, or artwork within the broader Chatters universe.

---

## Folder Structure

- **fundamental-2025/**  
  The foundational code and earliest behavioral materials for the series.  
  Released under **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**.

More folders will be added as new forms and scenes are created.

---

## Philosophy

Chatters explores how very small behaviors, tiny sounds, soft glows,  
simple pulses—can suggest a kind of life.  
The forms do not mimic animals or machines directly;  
instead, they inhabit their environments quietly,  
interacting with each other through timing, pitch, and light.

This repository preserves the evolution of these behaviors  
so they may be reused, reinterpreted, remixed, or studied freely.
